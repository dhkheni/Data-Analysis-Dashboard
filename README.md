# Vrinda-Store-Data-Analysis (Interactive Dashboard creation using MS Excel)
## Project Objective
The Vrinda Store wants to create an annual sales report for 2022.So that, the owner of the Vrinda store can understand their customers and grow more sales in 2023 and 2024.

# Questions / KPIs
The following business questions were analyzed:
Compare sales and orders using a single chart.
Which month generated the highest sales and orders?
Who purchased more — men or women?
What are the different order statuses in 2022?
Which are the top 10 states contributing to sales?
What is the relationship between age and gender based on the number of orders?
Which sales channel is contributing the maximum sales?
What is the highest-selling category?
What percentage of total orders were delivered?
Create an interactive dashboard to visualize the analysis.

# Dashboard

Monthly sales and order trends

Delivered orders
Age and gender analysis

# Project Insights
Based on the analysis, the following insights were identified:
Women customers are more likely to purchase products compared to men, accounting for approximately 65% of purchases.
Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top three states contributing to sales.
The adult age group (30–49 years) is the largest contributing customer segment, accounting for approximately 50% of orders.
Customers place the maximum number of orders through Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra.
More than 90% of products/orders were successfully delivered.

# Final Conclusion
To improve sales for Vrinda Store, a strategic marketing plan should focus on women customers aged 30–49 years in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
This demographic represents an important consumer segment and can be targeted through:
Digital marketing campaigns
Personalized promotions
Social media advertising
Targeted product recommendations
Special offers for the 30–49 age group

By focusing marketing efforts on these high-performing customer segments and sales channels, Vrinda Store can improve customer engagement and increase sales in the coming years.
